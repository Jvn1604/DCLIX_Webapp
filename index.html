Fees

What is the Fee Module?

The fee module handles every fee your club charges, from registration to grading. Set each fee up once in Fee Master, and D-Clix creates the invoices, collects payment online and records every receipt.

Fees you can charge

Registration feeMonthly feeTraining centre fee (special)Attendance feePackage feeGrading fee

Discounts you can give

Early payment discountReferral discount

What it does

Fee MasterOne place to set up every fee, from registration to grading.
PackagesCreate packages by number of classes, such as 5 classes a month, and set a fee for each package.
InvoicesD-Clix creates an invoice for each student, and parents see what is due on their home screen.
Online paymentParents pay by FPX from the app or the web, or submit a payment slip.
DiscountsReward early payers and referrals with discounts.
Receipts and reportsEvery payment gets a receipt. Parents can also pay ahead, and outstanding and collection reports show who has not paid.

Grading

What is the Grading Module?

The grading module takes a belt or grade test from start to finish: exam centres, examiners, the schedule, eligible students, fees, assessment, results and certificates.

What it does

Exam centres and examinersSet up exam centres and examiners, and tag each training centre to its exam centre.
Eligible studentsSelect who takes the grading, and check the unpaid and insufficient attendance lists first.
Grading feesA grading fee invoice is generated for each eligible student.
Assessment formExaminers record each student's assessment on the assessment form.
Results and beltsGrading results, certificates and belts are tracked for every student in the session.
Certificates and statisticsPrint certificates, and view exam result summaries and statistics.

Attendance

What is the Attendance Module?

The attendance module records who comes to every class, for both students and instructors. Mark attendance by QR code or by choosing names, save it, and report on it whenever you need.

What it does

QR check-inStudents scan the class QR code in the app, and coaches can also enter a QR code when marking attendance.
Mark attendanceChoose the date, training centre and time, add students one by one or all at once, then save.
Students and instructorsMark attendance for students or for instructors.
Training timesClasses follow each centre's training days and times, with a training type such as normal training.
Attendance reportsFilter by centre, time and date range, then export to Excel or PDF.
Linked to grading and feesAttendance feeds the insufficient attendance check for grading, and Fee Master has an attendance fee.
