No Refund Policy

Subscription payments for the D-Clix Club Management System

Draft for company approval. The live d-clix.com links to a No Refund Policy page that does not exist yet. This draft follows the company's published Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy; the company must review and approve it before launch.

1. All payments are final

All subscription fees paid for D-Clix are non-refundable. This includes monthly and yearly plans, any unused part of a subscription period, and plan downgrades made during a period.

The online payment gateway fee (RM 1.20 per online payment) is charged by the payment provider and is not refundable.

2. Cancelling your subscription

Your subscription renews as described in our Terms & Conditions. To stop it from renewing, give written notice at least 30 days before the renewal date.

If you end the agreement before the end of the agreed term, the early termination penalty in the Terms & Conditions applies. Cancelling does not create a refund for fees already paid.

3. When we will refund

We only refund payments in these cases:

a. Duplicate or incorrect charge — you were charged twice for the same invoice, or charged an amount different from your invoice. We refund the extra amount after checking it with the payment gateway.

b. Payment taken but account not activated — money left your bank or card but your club was not activated. We will either activate your account or refund the payment after checking it with the payment gateway.

c. Software we cannot fix — as set out in our warranty terms, if the Software does not perform as documented and we cannot correct it or provide an alternative, we may refund prepaid fees for the remaining subscription term of the affected Software.

d. Where the law requires it — nothing in this policy limits any right you have under Malaysian law.

4. How to request a refund

Contact us within 14 days of the payment with your club name, invoice or receipt number and the payment reference (shown on your receipt). Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 14 working days; your bank may take longer to show it.
