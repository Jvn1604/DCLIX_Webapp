Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Who we are

D-Clix Club Management System is operated by Raig Technologies (M) Sdn Bhd, Suite 442 Level 4 Block A, Kelana Centre Point, Jalan SS 7/19, SS7, 47301 Petaling Jaya, Selangor.

What we collect

  • Club registration: club name and code, head instructor, address, WhatsApp number, login email and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Billing: billing name, company, address, email and phone. Card and bank details are entered on our payment provider Billplz and never reach D-Clix.
  • Enquiries: name, phone, email, club name and your message.
  • Club data you add: students, guardians, instructors, attendance, payments and similar records that clubs manage in D-Clix.
  • Security logs: login times, IP address and browser, to protect accounts.

How we use it

To create and run your club account, process subscription payments, send receipts and service emails, respond to enquiries, provide support, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with providers that help run the service (payment processing by Billplz, hosting and email delivery) and when required by law.

Keeping data safe

Access is limited by role, passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and every change in the admin portal is recorded in an audit log.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent for marketing, by contacting us. Clubs are responsible for the student data they enter and should collect consent from students or guardians.
