Our story

Made for martial arts, not adapted from something else

What sets D-Clix apart is that its features are designed for martial arts clubs: belts and grading, classes, tournaments and fees, the way clubs really run them. On the web and in the mobile app, owners, coaches and members all get a modern, simple experience.

Built for martial arts

Belts, grading, tournaments and class schedules work the way your sport does.

Web and mobile

Admins work on the web dashboard; members and parents use the app.

Less admin

Registration, online payments and attendance tracking without paperwork.

A local team

A Malaysian company with a support team in Petaling Jaya.

In depth

Management tools to run your club to its full potential

See real-time updates on your club's performance, and manage registration, approval, attendance and payment from one dashboard.

  • Registration and approval of new members and instructors
  • Attendance by QR code scan or a manual update
  • Payments online by FPX, with invoices and receipts
